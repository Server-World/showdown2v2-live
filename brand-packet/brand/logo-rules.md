# SSL logo rules

## Canonical public league identity

Use the approved blue/orange **SSL shield + ball** identity for the public Supersonic Showdown League brand.

Canonical public assets live under `/assets/branding/`:

- `ssl-logo-primary.png` — primary compact public league mark
- `ssl-logo.svg` — shared website wrapper for the primary mark
- `ssl-social-card.svg` — social-preview treatment

The public identity is distinct from SSL Bot / bot-specific branding. **Do not replace the SSL Bot avatar or bot-specific identity with the public league mark.**

## Required public uses

- Twitch profile picture, profile banner, offline/video-player banner and channel panels
- Discord **server** icon/banner and other public server branding
- Website header/footer/favicon and public league graphics
- Social profile imagery and promotional creative
- Match/result graphics and other outward-facing league media
- Notion/internal brand references where the public league identity is being represented

## Do not

- Do not use older temporary small-car or legacy bot-logo treatments as the public league identity.
- Do not substitute the SSL Bot identity for the public league identity.
- Do not invent another SS badge.
- Do not distort, stretch or recolor the approved public mark.
- Do not place tiny text inside square avatars/icons.
