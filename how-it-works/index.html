Common questions

Before your first series.

Where do I register?

Join the Supersonic Showdown Discord and follow the league registration flow.

Where are official updates and rulings posted?

Discord is authoritative for registration, scheduling, announcements, transactions, disputes, eligibility decisions, and league actions.

Can a match be rescheduled?

Yes. Both teams must agree and the match must still be completed by the league deadline. League Operations handles exceptions.

Who reports results and replays?

The winning team reports the series score and the losing team confirms it. Required replay files are submitted within the league reporting window.
