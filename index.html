Divisions

Six tiers, so every match is worth playing

Placement is set by the bot from your tracked rating — not by self‑reporting. You move as your results move.

Mythic

The top of the ladder. The smallest field and the hardest schedule in the league.

Legend

Established duos with a track record. One tier below the ceiling, and closing.

Elite

Strong, consistent teams. Rotations are clean and mistakes get punished.

Contender

The competitive middle. Where most teams land and where the ladder is tightest.

Rookie

Newer duos finding their footing. Real matches, real stats, no sharks.

Amateur

The entry point. Learn the format, build a record, and climb from here.
