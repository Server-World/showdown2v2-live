How are regular-season ties in the standings broken?

The first tiebreaker is total Season Standing Points. The second is overall game win percentage, not series win percentage. The third is head-to-head record between the tied teams.

Can a match be rescheduled?

Yes. Both teams must agree to the reschedule and the match must still be completed by the league’s set match deadline. If the teams cannot agree, the originally scheduled match window remains in effect. League Operations handles exceptions and enforcement.

What are the basic substitution rules?

Substitutes must be eligible under league transaction rules. A Free Agent may substitute when the team remains under its MMR cap, and a lower-tier player may substitute upward within the same franchise. Players may not substitute downward, and another team’s rostered player may not serve as a substitute. Sub transactions must be submitted at least 30 minutes before the match set.

Who reports results and replays?

The winning team reports the series score and the losing team confirms it. Valid replays for games played must be submitted within 24 hours of the score report. When valid replays are missing, the game result can remain while the affected statistics may be forfeited under league rules.

Which maps are approved?

Beckwith Park, Champions Field, DFH Stadium, Forbidden Temple, and Drift Woods.

Which region is used?

US-East is the default unless both teams agree to another permitted region.
