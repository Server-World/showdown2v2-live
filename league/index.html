What maps are approved?

The current rulebook lists Beckwith Park, Champions Field, DFH Stadium, Forbidden Temple and Drift Woods.

What server region is used?

US-East is the default unless both teams agree to another permitted region.

Does the website decide standings or eligibility?

No. The website is a read-only publisher. SSL Bot/PostgreSQL remain authoritative for competitive facts.
