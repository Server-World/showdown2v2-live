Rulebook essentials

Competition rules at a glance.

How are regular-season ties broken?

Season Standing Points, then overall game win percentage, then head-to-head record between the tied teams.

What are the basic substitution rules?

Substitutes must be eligible under league transaction rules. A Free Agent may substitute when the team remains under its MMR cap, and a lower-tier player may substitute upward within the same franchise. Players may not substitute downward.

Which maps are approved?

Beckwith Park, Champions Field, DFH Stadium, Forbidden Temple, and Drift Woods.

Which region is used?

US-East is the default unless both teams agree to another permitted region.
